# App-building editorial research — October 30, 2026

Scope: five original English-language guides, approximately 5,000 words each, for the existing https://xroga.com/blog collection. The user explicitly authorized publishing this set to the live site. xroga.om in the request is treated as the established xroga.com domain.

## Method and limits

Qualitative public-search intent research, followed by current primary documentation checks. The keyword CSV contains 50 candidate terms per article; the master JSON contains 50 broader candidates. These are intent hypotheses, not measured search-volume or keyword-difficulty data. We have no independent traffic, backlinks, social shares, or virality analytics for competing sites. No claims of viral performance, guaranteed ranking, or guaranteed AI citations are made. Exact-match insertion of every term is deliberately not a content requirement.

## Competing content inspected

- Dyad, “Free AI App Builders Compared (2026)”: https://www.dyad.sh/blog/free-ai-app-builders-compared — free-plan comparison intent; vendor-authored, not independent testing. Our differentiation: cost categories, one-app evaluation worksheet, practical data/ownership checks. Its comparative plan numbers were not copied.
- Lovable, “Bolt vs Replit vs Lovable”: https://lovable.dev/guides/bolt-vs-replit-vs-lovable — product comparison intent; vendor perspective. Our differentiation: category decision framework, consistent trial and maintenance exercise rather than an unsupported universal ranking.
- Lovable, “How to build a real product”: https://docs.lovable.dev/tips-tricks/from-idea-to-app — tutorial intent. Our differentiation: an original fictional client-request tracker with explicit records, states, role checks and worked acceptance fixtures.
- Lovable prompting guide: https://docs.lovable.dev/prompting/prompting-one — planning and component-level prompt intent. Our prompt examples are original and include acceptance criteria.
- Bolt prompting guide: https://support.bolt.new/prompting/prompting-effectively — clearer prompting and troubleshooting intent. Used only to support a short description of the published guidance, not copied examples.

## Editorial and technical choices

Each guide has a direct introductory answer, exactly one original conceptual editorial image, 20+ sections, source citations, relevant internal links, and visible organization byline/date/read time. All prose is rendered on the server through the existing EditorialPage. No login or reading gate is added. Existing canonical URL inventory supplies sitemap and RSS. The optional llms.txt directory links these guides without claiming ranking benefit. Existing private-path crawler exclusions and preview noindex remain intact. No hidden keyword blocks, fabricated reviews, invented customer outcomes, or copied competitor prose.

The five images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool as original landscape editorial concepts, inspected, then compressed to WebP. The prompt set uses ivory backgrounds, cobalt glass and soft mint accents; subjects are an open builder toolbox, three app-building paths, sketch-to-app transformation, notebook-to-prompt transformation, and an app-card launch rocket. They are explicitly captioned as AI-generated conceptual illustrations, not product screenshots.
