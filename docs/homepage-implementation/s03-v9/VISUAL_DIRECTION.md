# S03 V9 — Visual direction

## Structure

S03 is one section with two chapters, each understandable on its own.

**Introduction.**
- Eyebrow, then the headline "Getting found is work. So is winning the customer.", then one 21-word supporting line.
- Two entrances, each an animated line illustration that previews its chapter:
  - "See what search engines miss."
  - "From search intent to a campaign ready for review."
- Each entrance jumps to its chapter, lands below the fixed header, and moves focus to the chapter heading. Scrolling is never hijacked.

**A · Search & Authority Studio: a precision-lit authority atlas.**
- Petrol-ink stage.
- Colour carries meaning: mint for opportunities, cyan for link provenance, periwinkle for AI citations, gold for local, warm red for problems.
- Layout: lenses on the left (228 px), stage in the middle, evidence inspector on the right (280 px).
- The providers for the active lens sit directly under the stage.

**B · Launch, Campaigns & Growth Studio: a warm creative atelier.**
- Graphite set, with warm product objects (the Ember Roasters pouch, drawn in code) and apricot accents.
- Layout: jobs on the left, canvas in the middle, settings and providers on the right.
- A ribbon under the canvas links each campaign to the customers it brings in, and opens Conversions with that channel selected.

**Brand glue.** Xroga blue appears only on focus rings and on the one route into the product ("Open the Xroga workspace"). The S00–S02 header and logo are untouched.

## Motion grammar

All motion uses `transform` and `opacity` only. Each animation plays once, with no loops.

**Opening.**
- The heading is readable immediately.
- The two entrances rise at 120 ms. The chapters fade in at 280 and 450 ms.
- Chapters fade only, with no transform, so the phone sheets inside them are never trapped.

**Links (the signature).**
- Verified edges draw from the source to the site (about 820 ms).
- Competitor-only edges arrive dashed at the competitor (600 ms, delayed 300 ms).
- A mention is an open line that stops short.
- Outreach draws a dashed path into a draft card. It never draws an edge to the site.

**Site health.** One tracer pass per "Inspect crawl", about 700 ms per link and staggered by depth. On the broken link the tracer stops halfway, in warm red.

**Selections and inspector.** Selections take 160–240 ms. The inspector arrives in 280 ms. Scene switches start at 50% opacity, so the stage is never blank.

**Campaigns.**
- Ad group: the search ad and landing page re-form together, and the message-match lines redraw.
- Placement: Feed and Story trade depth in 560 ms.
- Audience: the art re-tones in 520 ms.

**Reduced motion.** Every state appears at once: no tracer, no draw-ins, no entrance.

## What each reference taught, and how S03 differs

| Reference (clip and time) | Principle seen | How Xroga applies it | How Xroga differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daydream (0–5 s) | A few thin-outlined objects linked by flowing lines read as a system before any UI appears | The links graph: typed sources joined to the site and the competitor by lines whose shape carries meaning | Dark, typed and data-honest. Lines exist only where a relationship exists, and no link is ever "acquired". There are no hand-drawn illustrations and no peach palette. |
| Daydream (9–20 s) | A product proof surface inside a calm stage, with copy kept short | The evidence inspector: two lines, one state, one action | The inspector sits beside the work and changes with each selection; it is not a carousel |
| Peec AI (6.6 s, 18.4 s) | A focal fact first, depth behind it | Lens status lines ("3 link gaps") and two-line inspector titles | Answers, sources and states are shown inside one stage, not in a dashboard frame |
| Profound (6.8 s, 19.2 s) | Dark authority and confident typography | The petrol stage and editorial headings | No unlabelled constellation of dots |
| Clay (3.7–10.4 s) | Tactile objects with real material | The Ember pouch, editorial launch cards, warm paper surfaces | An original palette and artwork, with no mascot |
| Dub (4.2–11.6 s) | Motion that ties a channel to its outcome | The ribbon and Conversions strands ending in the product | Figures are always the selected channel's own figures |
