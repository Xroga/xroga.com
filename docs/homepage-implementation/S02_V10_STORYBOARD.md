## Category difference, shown not told

**Browser Automation** (one mission, executed now): near-frontal desktop, one application window, Xroga's blue
pointer acting on real controls, findings pinned where they happen, a result that waits for approval. The dock picks
which mission.

**AI Employees** (ongoing ownership, delivered later): the Xroga Workforce studio. No pointer. The story is a
lifecycle:

1. **Meet the team** (once, about 2.2 s): the parent orb unfolds into six specialists arranged in two tiers on a lit
   floor; each nameplate shows the responsibility and its schedule (↻ Every weekday · 07:00…).
2. **Give a job**: the chosen specialist steps forward; a two-step sheet sets Goal, When to work, What to deliver,
   then Allowed tools and Ask me before. "Preview assignment" (simulation only).
3. **While you're away**: the laptop lid closes; a playhead crosses Mon 17:42 → Tue 18:00 over six lanes; runs,
   inputs, deliverables and decisions land on each lane; the Research brief travels to Growth at 06:10; saved
   artifacts collect in a tray; "N waiting for your decision".
4. **Come back**: a roster with every employee's live status stays on the left; the open employee shows Delivered /
   Needs your decision / Next scheduled run, what changed, and their own tools with the decision on the object it
   concerns.

Reference cues (visual observations): MAUS (2) 9–14 s "Routines on a calendar" → the lanes timeline; MAUS (2)
21–23 s job-shaped results with a decision → desks; Grok 0–8 s and Cursor (2) 5–29 s persistent bot roster with
status lines → the return roster; founder's art reference (glass X with collar, gold tie bar, reflective floor and
orbit rings) → the atrium floor and rings.
