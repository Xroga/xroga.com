- Independent check of the Office files: opened once with openpyxl, python-docx and python-pptx in a temporary environment (headers, dates as dates, formats, a formula, freeze and filter, styles, a table, the slides); the environment was removed afterwards.
- Real browser: the Google Maps search address in the new skill returned the results feed with business names; the Hacker News and Substack JSON endpoints answered; Reddit refused a plain request, which the skill says.

## Not done, said plainly

- The upscaler is Swin2SR, not Real-ESRGAN, checked on one picture and slow (seconds per megapixel); an image over about 4 megapixels must be cropped or shrunk first.
- The SVG tracer produces polygons: good for logos and icons, rough on curves, and a photograph becomes a poster.
- `make_document` has no charts, images or footnotes in Word, no speaker notes in slides, and no formulas that are computed before saving (Excel computes them on opening). A .xlsx cell is limited to 32,767 characters, as in Excel.
- The domain skills are methods, not site scrapers: a site that changes or blocks needs the agent to read what is on the page. No lead or job tool sends anything.
- Not built from qz: scheduled automations, a model-helper tool, history, location, an MCP client.

## Third pass (2026-11-27, later the same day): the source, not only the documents

The owner said the first two passes were too shallow ("don't just read the README, read the whole repo"). The third pass read source for every repository and built what it found missing. What each repository's source gave, beyond the first table:

- **qz-browser-agent** (about 2,300 files): the whole kernel prompt, the page and wait tool contracts, the data store, the agent loop, the privacy and permission document. Built: **durable named tables** (the `table` tool: keyed add, pages, revisions, query, CSV, and `make_document` sheets with `fromTable`), because the rows of an extraction were replaced by the next extraction and a long collection had to pass through the model. Waits and tool timeouts now go up to an hour.
- **workflow-use**: the variable identifier and the healing code. Built without a model: demonstrations name the typed values that are inputs, with a `${placeholder}` each.
- **JobSpy**: the pay parsers of nine boards. Built: `parsePay` and the `data_ops` step `pay` (pay_min, pay_max, interval, currency, yearly equivalents, one comparable `pay_year`).
- **google-maps-scraper-kit** (concepts only): found that Maps fetches batches of places itself; confirmed in a real browser that the request exists. Built: hidden email decoding (Cloudflare `data-cfemail`, `[at]` and `[dot]` forms) in the page data reader, and the lead skill uses the network batches.
- **sharp**: `lib/operation.mjs` and the colour, channel and composite modules, function by function. Built: median, gamma, normalise, threshold, tint, negate, unflatten, channel extraction and the blend modes.
- **vtracer**: the corner-aware spline idea. Built: curved paths with corners kept.
- **manim**: the animation modules by name. Built: number counters and five more entrance effects (pulse, wiggle, flash, spin, drop).
- **OpenOutreach, Real-ESRGAN**: read as before; no new build.

Honest limit: qz alone has about 2,300 files and a full file-by-file reading of every repository was not possible in one session; the reading was by module, guided by what the extension lacked. The ledger rows say which parts were read.
