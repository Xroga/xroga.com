## What else was added from reading

- `read_file` and the article reader are announced to the model when a tab shows such a file, as the PDF reader already is.
- Bundled skill `reading-documents-articles-and-feeds` tells the model which reader to pick (article, data, read_file, read_pdf, untilEnd, holdMs) and what to quote back.
- The skill ranker test now checks every bundled skill is first for at least one request, against the catalog size instead of a fixed 19.

## Test evidence

- Unit: `page-data.test.ts` (5), `file-format.test.ts` (13: docx lists and tables, xlsx dates and formulas and real row numbers and sheet by name, pptx order, EPUB chapters, CSV dialects and profile and paging, zip member, JSON, feed, find, three kinds of refusal including a bomb and too many members), `acceptance-scorers.test.ts` (8), skill trigger cases for the four new skills. Whole `src/agent` folder: 104 files, 776 tests pass; type check clean.
- Chromium, real extension, through the autorun harness: `S4-article-data-hold` (story without menu and ads, data with JSON-LD, meta, mails, phones, socials, feed, microdata; a quick click releases early and a 1.4 s hold reveals the code), `S4-read-file` (every format from files served over HTTP, a 404 and a PDF named plainly), `S4-infinite-scroll` (a feed that loads 10 reports at a time: one scroll gets a batch, `untilEnd` gets all 60 and the page says no more appeared). All three pass.
- Full Chromium scenario suite: 347 of 348 passed. The one failure, S4-complex-workflow, was an empty text-recognition read under load (the known flake); it passes alone on the same build.
