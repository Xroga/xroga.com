# Xroga Living X-Companion

## Brand source

The supplied transparent Xroga orb is stored at `frontend/public/brand/xroga-companion-source.png`. Optimized 512 px and 192 px WebP variants are used locally. Runtime surfaces never hotlink the source.

## Silhouette exploration

Three original silhouettes were evaluated and remain available as customizer costumes:

1. **Core guardian** — compact proportions, strong head/body separation, luminous protected X core, and the clearest small-size silhouette.
2. **Builder** — wider modular torso and stronger articulated arms for repository, coding, build, and repair gestures.
3. **Navigator** — larger rounded head and mantle-forward shape for guidance, reading, and planning states.

The core guardian is the default because it remains legible at the 58 px composer size. Builder and Navigator alter real SVG layers; they are not separate static images.

## Visual and behavioral rules

- The supplied orb is the energy core; the X-shaped crown reinforces the same identity.
- Face, crown, body, core, arms, mantle, legs, scan line, and operation orbit are separate SVG layers.
- Mood describes expression. Operation describes real system activity. Neither is used to imply sentience.
- Runtime gestures are driven by Xroga composer and swarm events. Silent keepalives cannot create activity.
- Speech is opt-in, uses accessible text as the source, and speaks only real assistant output or an explicitly requested deterministic status.
- Beige, White, Gray, and Black themes adapt contrast without changing the brand identity.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential animation.
- Weekly code energy is optional, cannot block work, and can be reduced or disabled.
