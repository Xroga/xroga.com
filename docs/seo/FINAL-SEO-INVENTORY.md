# Final SEO inventory

Updated: September 11, 2026.

## Indexable architecture

- Commercial: homepage, AI app builder, AI coding agent, AI website builder, pricing, features, integrations.
- Evaluation: `/compare` and its evidence-backed comparisons; `/alternatives` and workflow-specific alternatives.
- Jobs to be done: `/build` guides and `/build-with` provider guides.
- Technology: `/stack` with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Python guides.
- Migration: `/migrate` with Lovable, Bolt, Replit, and v0 migration guides.
- Education and evidence: `/learn`, `/blog`, `/research`, `/showcase`, `/docs`, `/security`, and `/changelog`.
- Utility: `/tools/production-readiness-checker`, a working client-side checklist with a visible methodology and no stored input.

Every published detail route is generated from a maintained content registry, linked from its hub, self-canonical, and emitted in the sitemap. Reserved route factories remain unpublished until a complete record exists.

## Deliberately non-indexable

Authenticated workspace, dashboard, settings, admin, auth, API, share-token, embedded preview, and terminal surfaces remain outside the public search architecture. Legacy aliases redirect to their canonical replacement and are not listed in the sitemap.

## Structured data

The site uses Organization, Person, WebSite, SoftwareApplication, WebPage/CollectionPage, Article, and BreadcrumbList where visible content supports them. Unsupported ratings, reviews, invented awards, and generic FAQ rich-result markup are absent.
