# Competitor facts refresh protocol

Comparison and migration claims are maintained in `frontend/src/lib/seoGrowthContent.ts` and `frontend/src/lib/seoExpansionContent.ts`.

## Monthly review

1. Open every linked official vendor source.
2. Verify only the exact claims used: repository import/export, branching, hosting/deployment, supported project types, and published pricing mechanics.
3. Remove or rewrite claims whose source moved, became ambiguous, or no longer supports the wording.
4. Update the `lastVerified`/`reviewed` date only for records actually reviewed.
5. Run content tests, production build, and the live SEO audit.

Do not copy vendor marketing superlatives, infer unavailable limits, or present Xroga as universally better. Comparisons must identify when another product may be the better fit and link to primary documentation.
