# Crypto editorial distribution — prepared, not submitted

These five new guides are published on Xroga's own site. External backlinks and guest posts require a third-party editor's consent and publication; none should be recorded as earned until a public URL exists. Do not buy links, use undisclosed placements, or imply that official documentation endorses Xroga.

## Guest-post pitches

1. **Developer education publication:** “The blockchain app brief that catches wallet and RPC failures before launch.” Offer a practical worksheet drawn from the [blockchain app guide](/blog/how-to-build-blockchain-app-with-ai). Keep the article educational; link to the guide only when editorially useful.
2. **Wallet UX community:** “Why wallet connection is the wrong first screen for many Web3 products.” Include a real usability test script and an explicit distinction between connecting, signing and transacting. Supporting guide: [wallet UX](/blog/smart-wallet-ux-account-abstraction).
3. **Payments engineering newsletter:** “A stablecoin checkout needs an order ledger before it needs a Pay button.” Provide a state diagram and duplicate-event test scenario. Supporting guide: [stablecoin payments](/blog/stablecoin-payment-app-guide).
4. **Smart contract security community:** “What a green build does not prove about a dApp.” Focus on asset inventory, privileges and testable invariants, with official Ethereum and OWASP references. Supporting guide: [security checklist](/blog/smart-contract-security-checklist).
5. **Data/analytics publication:** “Every crypto dashboard metric needs a source, unit and freshness label.” Include a metric-definition template. Supporting guide: [dashboard data](/blog/crypto-dashboard-data-guide).

## Editorial outreach template

Subject: Practical guest article idea for your readers — [specific topic]

Hello [editor], I enjoyed your coverage of [specific published piece]. Your readers may find a worked example of [precise problem] useful. I can contribute an original, non-promotional article with a diagram/checklist, source links to official technical documentation, and clear caveats about what still requires human review. Proposed outline: [three specific headings]. If that fits your editorial calendar, I can send a complete draft. If it does not, no follow-up is needed.

Before sending, verify the publication's current contributor policy, audience fit and disclosure rules. Record contacted editor, permission, date, response and final published URL separately. A pitch is not a backlink; a published article is not automatically a ranking signal.

## Internal linking and measurement

The `/crypto` landing page links to all five guides. Each guide links back to `/crypto` and to relevant sibling guides. The blog hub and sitemap include the new URLs automatically. After publication, check Search Console indexing and search queries over time; no traffic, ranking or AI-answer visibility should be claimed without observed data.
