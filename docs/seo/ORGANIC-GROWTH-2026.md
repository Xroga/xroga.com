# Organic growth operating plan — 2026

## Objective

Earn qualified discovery by making Xroga’s real product model easier to understand, evaluate, test, and cite. The plan does not promise rankings or traffic.

## 0–30 days

1. Reauthenticate Search Console and verify domain ownership, sitemap processing, index coverage, and priority URLs.
2. Establish GA4 activation events: pricing view, signup start, successful signup, repository connection, first accepted task, validated build, and verified deployment. Never send prompts, source code, tokens, or personal data as event parameters.
3. Review Clarity for layout friction and dead interactions on the landing, pricing, comparison, and signup paths.
4. Request indexing only for newly shipped or materially changed canonical URLs.
5. Publish one first-party proof asset derived from a reproducible repository task, with commit, checks, limitations, and date.

## 31–90 days

1. Refresh competitor facts monthly from official documentation and record material changes.
2. Expand only clusters with impressions, useful engagement, or recurring sales/support questions.
3. Turn real release evidence into case studies, technical notes, and short videos with matching watch pages.
4. Earn relevant links through useful tools, original tests, maintainers’ resources, and editorial outreach; do not buy links or automate spam.
5. Review crawl errors, content overlap, orphaning, canonical drift, and stale claims in the weekly automated audit.

## Decision gates

- Keep: the page answers a distinct need and shows useful engagement or assists a conversion path.
- Improve: it has relevant impressions but weak satisfaction, unclear evidence, or internal-link gaps.
- Consolidate: its purpose substantially overlaps another canonical page.
- Remove/noindex: it is private, empty, expired, unsupported, or cannot be made genuinely useful.
