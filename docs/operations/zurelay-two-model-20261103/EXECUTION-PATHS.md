## Historical references

`models.ts` retains old identifiers, prices and provider destinations to interpret existing financial history. `quota.ts` retains historical pool aliases. `userProviderKeys.ts` retains old encrypted credential namespaces without making their models dispatchable. Public identity redaction and error classification retain historical provider names. These references are not executable generation routes. No historical key was revoked or read during this work.

The catalog, SDK destination checks and before-dispatch guards independently reject unapproved models. Raw strings and a valid model-looking response never grant actor, account, spend or current-worker authority. Missing usage after dispatch preserves uncertainty, without another request.

## Recovery limitation

The current cd5541 image is a preserved recovery asset, but it cannot certify the new Zurelay policy. A future Zurelay rollout must have a reviewed compatible recovery image or keep model admission and the queue closed during rollback. Never silently reactivate historical providers to recover a Zurelay release. Preserve voice routing, snapshots and all ledger holds.
